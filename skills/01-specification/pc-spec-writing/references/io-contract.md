# Input and Output Contract Notes

## Inputs

- **requirements-doc** -- Required reviewed behavior, scope, and non-goals. Reuse equivalent approved requirement sections or a current-behavior baseline for a brownfield specification; do not rerun a producer solely to rename the input.
- **domain-model** -- Conditional: required domain terms/invariants when they affect the contract. Record missing domain decisions for the responsible owner rather than inventing a model.

## Outputs

- **spec-doc** -- Versioned contract and its approval stage, with constraints and owned questions for architecture, acceptance design, and implementation. Unresolved questions cannot authorize dependent implementation, even when the document is ready for architecture review.
