# Anti-Pattern Notes

1. **Performative agreement** -- sounding agreeable before verifying whether the comment is even correct.
2. **External-reviewer obedience** -- treating outside feedback as an order instead of a hypothesis.
3. **Partial understanding, partial implementation** -- changing a dependent group while its contract remains unclear, or blocking unrelated authorized corrections without an actual dependency.
4. **Batch-and-pray** -- applying unrelated risky changes together without evidence that isolates their effects; coherent root-cause batches may share verification.
5. **Context amnesia** -- implementing a suggestion that breaks an upstream architecture, contract, or compatibility constraint.
