# Input and Output Contract Notes

## Inputs

- **review-report**, **retrospective-report**, or **postmortem-report** -- Alternative sources of concrete debt evidence, not a mandatory bundle. A current documented workaround or delivery finding also suffices when it identifies the cost/risk, affected boundary, and source.

## Outputs

- **tech-debt-registry** -- Evidence, impact/recurrence, priority, and ownership or pending ownership decision; start with a baseline when no history exists.
- **remediation-plan** -- Next action, success signal, effort uncertainty, and proposed/committed capacity. Planning consumes this proposal; the registry itself does not allocate people or waive a blocking defect.
