# Input and Output Contract Notes

## Inputs

- **incident-timeline**, **postmortem-report**, or **review-report** -- Use those relevant to the completed work. A normal sprint/release can instead start from delivery outcomes, coordination evidence, stakeholder feedback, and previous actions. No incident report is required for work without an incident.

## Outputs

- **retrospective-report** -- Scope, observed outcomes, supported explanations, effective practices, and unresolved questions.
- **improvement-actions** -- Evidence-backed changes with owner, success signal, timing/decision needed, and next route for planning or intake. May be empty with a reason; it does not grant scope or capacity approval.
