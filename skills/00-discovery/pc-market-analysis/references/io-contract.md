# Input and Output Contract Notes

## Inputs

None required. This skill can begin from the project context alone.

## Outputs

- **market-research-report**: Relevant alternatives, supported gaps or a no-supported-opportunity conclusion, decision-relevant estimates/unknowns, and the ranked options or no-entry/further-research recommendation. Include source dates and limits. Feasibility and research consume this context; an untested opportunity remains a hypothesis.
