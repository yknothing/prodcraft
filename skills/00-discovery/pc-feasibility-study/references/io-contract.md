# Input and Output Contract Notes

## Inputs

- **intake-brief** -- Minimum: a concrete proposed change, expected value, constraints, and the viability decision. For existing systems, use its references to current code, operations, cost, and ownership evidence.
- **problem-frame** -- Optional direction and unresolved assumptions from framing.
- **market-research-report** -- Conditional when demand or competitive position determines viability; internal changes need no manufactured market report.
- **user-persona-set** -- Optional evidence about affected users and adoption constraints.

## Outputs

- **feasibility-report** -- Alternatives, evidence, applicable viability dimensions, uncertainty, recommendation, and needed decision authority. Requirements and architecture consume approved constraints; unresolved viability assumptions remain explicit in planning.
